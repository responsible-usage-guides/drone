# Quick Flight Checklist

**A quick reference for operators in the field.**

**[Download the printable flight checklist (PDF)](https://docs.drone.hotosm.org/manuals/dji-mini-4-5-pro-checklist.pdf)** - a one-page checklist to print on A6, laminate, and keep in your drone bag. It's written for the DJI Mini 4 Pro and Mini 5 Pro, but most steps apply to any small drone.

The list below covers the wider steps, from coordination and permissions through to backing up your photos.

---

> **Unsure about even one item? Postpone the flight.**

<div class="checklist">

## Before leaving base

- Flight registered with the aerial assessment coordinator (or local authorities, if there isn't one)
- Flight authorization obtained, and NOTAMs checked
- Manufacturer geofence zones checked, and unlocked if needed (separate from flight authorization)
- Local authorities and community or camp leaders informed
- Area checked on satellite imagery or online maps; take-off spots identified
- Sensitive sites excluded or agreed safeguards in place
- Weather forecast checked for the flight window (ideally 10am-2pm)
- Firmware and apps updated
- Batteries charged, with spares for the planned area
- Memory card inserted (U3 or faster) and tested
- Flight plan ready: altitude, at least 70% overlap (more for trees, sand, or water), camera at ~80°, flying across the wind

## On site

- Take-off spot is flat, clear, and away from people and traffic, with a clear view over the flight area
- No crowds, power lines, or obstacles in the flight area
- Wind light (below ~20 km/h) and sky stable
- High-visibility vests on; observer ready
- Drone undamaged; propellers clean and intact; gimbal cover removed
- Battery not swollen or damaged
- Controller on first, then drone
- Drone battery above 90%, controller above 60%
- Strong GPS signal; no error messages
- Camera: photo mode, 4:3, 12 MP, JPEG, fast shutter, **zoom at 1x**
- Return to Home altitude set above obstacles; signal-loss action checked
- Short test hover: drone responds normally
- "Home point updated" confirmed

## During flight

- Photos start at the first waypoint, not at take-off
- Clear view of the sky over the flight area (observer watching)
- Battery, altitude, and camera view monitored
- Zoom still at 1x
- Photos stop as soon as the last line is done
- Return home if battery runs low or anything seems wrong

## After flight

- Drone off, then controller off
- Battery removed: warm, not hot, and not swollen
- Drone checked for damage
- Photos reviewed on site: full coverage, sharp, and at 1x
- Photos backed up before anything else
- Notes for the end-of-day debrief

</div>

## In an emergency

| Situation                       | Action                                    |
| :------------------------------ | :---------------------------------------- |
| Rain or strong wind coming      | Press Return to Home and land             |
| Need to stop and think          | Pause; drone hovers in place              |
| Disoriented                     | Let go of both sticks; most drones hover with a good GPS signal |
| Bird approaching                | Fly straight up; return when safe         |
| Plane or helicopter nearby      | Descend and land straight away            |

A more detailed version, with full pre-flight and post-flight inspections, is in the [Drone Tasking Manager docs](https://docs.drone.hotosm.org/manuals/flight-checklists/).
